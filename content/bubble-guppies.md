- **Molly**: The bubbly, musical co-host and natural leader of the show who loves to sing and perform.
- **Gil**: The energetic, silly, and adventurous co-host who is Molly's best friend and gets easily excited.
- **Goby**: The imaginative, creative, and artistic guppy who loves storytelling and outdoor adventures.
- **Deema**: A high-energy and goofy character with big hair who loves being the center of attention.
- **Oona**: The sweetest and most empathetic member of the group who cares deeply about others, animals, and plants.
- **Nonny**: The brainy, cautious, and analytical boy in the group who is smart but more reserved.
- **Zooli**: An animal expert who joins the main group starting in Season 5 and loves sea horses.
